Overview & context

Playback, licensing, and personalization must be designed together.

The project spans desktop and mobile layouts, local and online sources, persistent playback state, preview and download licenses, and cold-start recommendations.

Problem

A traditional single-window player does not adapt well across platforms. Opaque cloud profiles undermine privacy, while media sources require clear boundaries between preview, cache, and permanent download.

PRODUCT GOALUseful personalization without an account

Favorites, history, paths, and preference signals remain on the device by default.

CONTENT BOUNDARYMedia behavior follows the license

iTunes is preview-only; Internet Archive downloads are limited to openly licensed material.

Architecture

Separate interface, application use cases, domain models, and data sources.

Ports isolate playback, media sources, persistence, and recommendation logic for testability.

Technical decisions

Privacy, licensing, and explainability drive the architecture.

01

Flutter rebuild

One UI layer covers four platforms while preserving platform-specific file, storage, and playback capabilities.

02

Source adapters

Metadata search is separated from actual playback through licensed providers.

03

Thresholded personalization

Personalization starts only after enough useful signals have accumulated.

04

Time decay

Older behavior decays over a 45-day half-life, with freshness, repetition, and artist-diversity adjustments.
